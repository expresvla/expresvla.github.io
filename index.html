ExpReS-VLA: Specializing Vision-Language-Action Models Through Experience Replay and Retrieval

Shahram Najam Syed1*, Yatharth Ahuja1*, Arthur Jakobsson1, Jeffrey Ichnowski1
1Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University
Accepted to ICRA 2025
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation 2025
*Equal contribution
Paper arXiv Video Lab

ExpReS-VLA specializes a VLA on-device with compressed experience replay and RAG.

Abstract

Vision-language-action models generalize broadly, but a deployed robot needs reliable performance on a few tasks in one environment. ExpReS-VLA adapts a pretrained VLA on-device with a compressed replay buffer and retrieval-augmented training, without catastrophic forgetting.

Frozen SigLIP and DINOv2 embeddings cut storage by 97% versus raw image-action pairs. Cosine RAG injects the most similar past successes and failures into each batch. Thresholded Hybrid Contrastive Loss (THCL) learns from unsuccessful attempts. Adaptation takes 31 seconds from 12 demonstrations on one RTX 5090.

Method

A frozen encoder writes experiences into a dual success/failure buffer. Top-k retrieval and a prioritizer update the policy on an edge GPU.

Results

On LIBERO, spatial success rises from 82.6% to 93.1% and long-horizon from 61% to 72.3%. On a Franka, in-distribution and OOD success both reach 98%.
